### I. Performance And Memory First

**Conforms.**

- **Input path.** Nothing is copied: arrays are borrowed through `slice::from_raw_parts`

  listed in Complexity Tracking (plan.md:269-270).

- **Solver configuration audit** (research R2, R9). I re-checked the vendor facts:
  - the Gurobi 13.0 `SoftMemLimit` entry matches the quoted text, including "allocated memory is
    tracked across all models within a Gurobi environment" and "termination may be

  - the entry states no restriction on when the parameter may be set;
  - `gurobi_c.h` declares `GRB_DBL_PAR_SOFTMEMLIMIT` (line 1201) and `GRB_MEM_LIMIT 17`

### VII. Rust And FFI Coding Standards

**Partly conforms. One defect (finding B).**

- **VII-A.** `unsafe` is confined to `src/capi/`, which is admitted as an FFI module beside the

**Finding B (VII-B with X): an unmarked inference underlies the Gurobi `Send`.**

- Research R3 (research.md:56-58) quotes the vendor's instruction "In a multithreaded
  application, create and delete environments in the main thread".
- The decision nevertheless lets an `Env` be created and dropped on any thread, serialised by a
  lock (research.md:47-49, 62-66). With `Env: Send`, a Rust caller can drop the `Env`, and so call
  `GRBfreeenv`, on a worker thread. A C caller can do the same through `optsolver_env_free`.
- I read the article: it gives no reason for the main-thread instruction, and does not say whether
  serialisation by a lock may stand in for it.
- The design therefore rests on an inference: the instruction exists only because creation and
  deletion are not thread-safe, so excluding concurrency is enough. The `SAFETY` argument of
  `unsafe impl Send` will rely on that inference, but the artefacts neither state it nor mark it
  unverified. VII-B requires vendor support, and X requires unverified claims to be marked.

A related point to settle under the same repair:

- `GRBnewmodel` gives each model its own copy of the environment, and `GRBfreemodel` frees it
  (`GRBgetenv`, `src/gurobi/ffi.rs:189`; used at `src/gurobi/solve.rs:136`).
- The planned lock covers `Env::new` and `Drop` only.
- Research says the lock "closes a hazard that existed before". Whether the vendor's "creation and
  deletion" also covers these model copies is unexamined.

## Verdict

**REVISE_ARCHITECTURE (FAIL).**

The requirements stand. The architecture is unchanged in substance and sound: the names are
approved, the reuse assessment is complete, there is no duplication, and reachability and adoption
are in order. But the ADR that would be accepted with this delta records the opposite of the
delta's `Send` decision. In addition, the vendor grounding of the Gurobi `Send` rests on an
inference that is not marked.

**Binding constraint**: the ADR and every planning artefact state one thread decision.
- `gurobi::Env` and `mosek::Env` become `Send` and not `Sync`.
- Gurobi creation and freeing are serialised in `gurobi::env`.
- The Gurobi vendor grounding, including its "main thread" instruction, is either verified or
  explicitly marked as an inference with a check routed to tasks.

### Violations and repairs

**A. ADR-0004 contradicts the delta and the spec (XIII-C, VII-B, XV-E).**
- In decision 5, replace "The Rust `Env` types stay `!Send`" with the decision taken: `Send`, not
  `Sync`, citing feature 005 research R3, and superseding feature 001 R15 and feature 002 R2.
- In decision 1, list the `Send` change beside `Options::memory_limit_gb` as a Rust-API change.

**B. Unmarked inference underlying `unsafe impl Send` for `gurobi::Env` (VII-B, X).** In research
R3 (and briefly in ADR decision 5):
- state that the vendor instructs creation and deletion "in the main thread" without giving a
  reason;
- state that the design reads this as a requirement to exclude concurrent creation and deletion,
  which the lock provides;
- mark that reading unverified.

Then route a check to the task list. Options include asking Gurobi support or recording a
licensed probe of creation and freeing on non-main threads. If neither can be had, keep the
reading as an accepted, recorded risk, with that recorded in the `SAFETY` comment. Alternatively,
narrow the documented thread rule.

In the same place, state whether model-environment copies (`GRBnewmodel`, `GRBfreemodel`) fall
under the vendor's statement and need the lock. Mark the answer verified or unverified.

**C. Keys used in place of names (XII-A).** Add the descriptive name beside each bare key at
plan.md:55, 117, 124, 125 and 208.

**D. NaN-fill size stated inconsistently.** Change research.md:117 to O(m + n + k) writes, to
agree with the plan and the contract.

After these repairs a new reviewer with a fresh context reviews the result (VI-A). Nothing else in
this review blocks `APPROVE_WITH_ADR` once they are made.
